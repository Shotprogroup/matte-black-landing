Questions

Things people ask before they book.

Anything else, email us and we will answer properly.

Do you only work on supercars?

No. We have wrapped and protected everything from daily drivers to Ferraris, Porsches and Lamborghinis. If the car matters to you, it gets the same care.

Will a wrap damage my paint?

Quality vinyl, installed and removed properly, protects the paint underneath. Most cars come out of a wrap in better condition than they went in.

What is the difference between PPF and a wrap?

Paint protection film is a clear, self-healing layer for protection. A wrap is coloured vinyl for changing the look. They can be combined, with film over a wrap, for the best of both.

How long does it take?

It depends on the car and the job. A full wrap or full-body film typically takes several days in the studio. We confirm timing with your quote.

Can I see the design before committing?

Yes. Every project starts with a photoreal 3D render of your car in the colour, finish or livery you are considering. Revise it until it is right.

Where are you?

Melbourne, by appointment. We send the studio address with your booking.
